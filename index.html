    FAQ

    Questions buyers ask before they book.

    Still unsure? Ask us directly on the call. No sales script, just answers.

    What is AI engine optimisation?
    AI engine optimisation (AEO) structures your site, content and entity data so assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity understand your brand and cite it when buyers ask for recommendations. It works alongside classic SEO, not instead of it.
    How long does a brand and website project take?
    Most brand plus website engagements launch in 7 to 11 weeks. Custom AI software usually ships a working pilot in 6 weeks and a production release in 14 to 20 weeks.
    Is our data safe when you build AI software?
    Yes. We deploy inside your cloud or on premises, encrypt data at rest and in transit, never train shared models on your data, and follow SOC 2 and ISO 27001 aligned controls. We sign your NDA and DPA before discovery starts.
    Do you only work with large enterprises?
    No. Around a third of our work is with founders launching new companies. You get the same senior team and standards our Fortune 500 clients get, scoped to your stage.
    Can we hire you for just one service?
    Absolutely. Many clients start with a brand refresh or an AEO audit, then expand. Everything is designed to connect later without rework.
    How is pricing structured?
    Fixed price per phase, agreed before work starts. No hourly surprises. Growth and support retainers are monthly and can be cancelled with 30 days notice.
    Who owns the work when we finish?
    You do. Brand assets, source files, code and trained model weights transfer to you in full on final payment.
    What if we are not happy with the first concepts?
    We revise until the direction is right. If the discovery phase does not meet your expectations, we refund that phase in full.
